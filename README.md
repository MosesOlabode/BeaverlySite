# Beaverly

Beaverly builds **Chilla**, your financial worker.

**Chilla works towards your goals in the financial markets and reports back.** It works within the boundaries you approve.

Chilla is designed to carry supported financial-market work through supported trading accounts while you remain in control of the material choices that belong to you. Beaverly is non-custodial: market funds remain with the provider hosting the trading account.

Beaverly is a software company, not a brokerage, bank, custodian, investment adviser or portfolio manager. Chilla does not guarantee profits or remove market risk.
