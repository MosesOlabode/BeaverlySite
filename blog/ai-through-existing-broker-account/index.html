AI + connected accounts

Can AI work through my existing broker account?

Yes, when the account and provider are supported. Your funds do not need to move to the AI platform just because you want software to do ongoing market work.

September 12, 20265 min read

There are two very different trust models hidden inside the phrase “automated trading.”

In one model, you send money to the automation platform itself. In another, the software works through an account you already have with an external provider.

The second model is often called non-custodial automation.

What does non-custodial mean here?

It means the automation company is not the place where your market funds are held.

Your funds stay with the external financial account provider you selected. The automation product connects to that supported account so it can carry out the work you authorize.

Chilla follows this model. Beaverly does not become the custodian of the money in your connected account.

Does connecting my account give the AI unlimited access?

No. A provider connection and authorization for live Chilla work are separate things.

Connecting a supported account makes that account available to the product. Before material live work begins, you review the work Chilla has prepared and authorize what should run.

After authorization, Chilla can continue working inside those boundaries without asking you to approve every individual market action.

Can Chilla use an account I already have?

If the account is compatible with a supported Chilla connection path, yes. Exact compatibility depends on the provider, account type, region and the markets available through that account.

The practical first step is to check Beaverly's current compatibility page or let Chilla guide you when you are ready to go live.

What if I do not have a suitable account yet?

You can start in Chilla Demo without connecting an external live account.

When you are ready for live work, Chilla can also help navigate supported provider setup journeys. Sensitive steps such as passwords, OTP/MFA, identity checks, legal declarations and account approvals remain yours.

Can Chilla withdraw money from the account?

Beaverly does not take custody of your funds, and provider-account administration has a separate user-control boundary.

Chilla may help you reach a supported deposit, withdrawal, transfer or settings area. Choosing transaction details and confirming a money-moving action remains with you.

Why does this distinction matter?

Custody changes the trust relationship.

If a platform holds your money, you are trusting it both as the software doing the work and as the entity holding the funds. With non-custodial automation, those responsibilities stay separate: the provider holds the account funds, while the software performs the authorized automation role.

Non-custodial does not mean risk-free. Market losses can still occur, providers can fail or become unavailable, and software can encounter problems. It simply answers one important question clearly: where does the money live?

What should I check before connecting an account?

  • Is my provider/account type supported?
  • Where do my funds remain after connection?
  • Does connecting the account automatically activate market activity?
  • What work must I review and authorize separately?
  • Can I pause or stop active work?
  • Can I revoke or disconnect provider access?
  • Who controls deposits, withdrawals and transfers?

How Chilla approaches it

Chilla is designed so the relationship starts with your goal rather than with a provider dashboard.

You can begin in demo. When you choose to go live, you connect a supported account, review the material work Chilla prepares, authorize it, and let Chilla work within those boundaries.

Your funds stay with the provider. Chilla works towards your goals in the financial markets and reports back.
