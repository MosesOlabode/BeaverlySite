Can AI actually do things for me online?

You've asked AI what to do. Can it go and do some of the work too? Yes—if the AI has the right tools, it can work beyond the chat.

October 10, 20265 min read

Most people have tried asking AI a question. It explains something, offers suggestions and tells you what to click next. That can be useful. But what if you want help with the clicking too?

Some AI assistants can use websites on your behalf. Depending on the task and the tools available, they can research options, move between pages and help with everyday online tasks. You describe what you want done, just as you would in a conversation.

“Find me a good laptop within my budget.” “What do I need to renew my passport?” “Help me get started with an account.”

Can AI use websites for me?

Yes, when the assistant has a browser or computer-use feature. It can inspect pages, follow useful links, compare information and sometimes navigate the steps of a task. What it can do depends on the website and what its tools permit.

Researching something to buy is different from paying for it. Finding official application requirements is different from submitting an application. You still benefit when you don't have to work out every next step on your own.

What could I ask it to help with?

Start with a real problem rather than a technical instruction. For example: “Compare a few phones that have a good camera without spending too much.” Or: “I need to renew my passport. Find the official process and help me prepare.”

You can also ask about money goals: “I want to save enough for a new place” or “I want to grow my money without watching charts all day.” A useful AI assistant should understand what you want, identify the work it can actually help with and bring you the decisions that matter.

When would I need to take over?

Some steps belong to you: passwords, identity verification, legal declarations, payment details and important confirmations. Websites can also change, refuse an action or require extra verification.

A good assistant should make progress where it can, hand you the screen when needed and tell you what it did, rather than pretending every task was completed.
