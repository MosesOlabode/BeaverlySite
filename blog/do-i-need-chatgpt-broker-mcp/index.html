AI agents + trading accounts

Do I need to connect ChatGPT or Claude to my broker?

No. Bringing your own AI assistant to a broker is one model. Chilla is a different one: the financial worker itself.

September 13, 20265 min read

AI and brokerage accounts are starting to meet in a few different ways. One increasingly common approach is to connect a general AI assistant — such as ChatGPT or Claude — to a broker that supports AI-agent access.

That can be useful. It is also not the only way to use conversational AI for market work.

What does “connect my AI to my broker” mean?

In that model, a broker offers a supported way for an external AI assistant to work with brokerage information or actions. You keep using the AI product you already know while the broker remains the account provider.

The AI assistant and the brokerage account remain separate products. You choose the AI, connect it to the broker, and work inside that assistant.

Where does Chilla fit?

Chilla is not a connector you install into ChatGPT, Claude or another general AI assistant.

Chilla is the financial worker you talk to directly. You tell Chilla what you are working towards. Chilla handles the supported product complexity, works within the boundaries you approve, and reports back.

When live work needs a trading account, you use a supported trading account with Chilla. Your funds remain with the provider hosting that account.

So which model is simpler?

It depends on what you want.

If you already have a favourite general AI assistant and want to configure it around a broker connection, a bring-your-own-agent setup may fit you well.

If you want one product whose job is specifically to carry supported financial-market work, Chilla is built for that relationship. You do not need to choose an external AI host first and then assemble the experience around it.

Do I need to build a strategy first?

Not with Chilla. You can start from what you are working towards rather than arriving with trading code, indicator rules or a strategy specification.

When a material choice still belongs to you, Chilla brings it into the conversation and can explain the supported options simply.

Can Chilla help with the account setup itself?

For supported providers, yes. Chilla can use the web to help with trading-account setup and routine account-management navigation.

Sensitive or consequential steps stay with you: passwords, OTP or MFA, identity checks, legal declarations, account approvals, transaction details and money-moving confirmations.

Does connecting a trading account give Chilla unlimited authority?

No. Connecting a trading account and authorizing live Chilla work are separate things.

Chilla works within the boundaries you approve. Material changes that need you come back to you, while ongoing work inside an already authorized scope does not need a fresh click for every individual market action.

What about Demo?

You do not have to connect a live trading account just to experience Chilla.

Chilla Demo follows real market prices while the balance and execution are simulated inside Chilla. The simulated balance cannot be withdrawn and no live order is sent to an external provider.

The short answer

No, you do not need to connect ChatGPT, Claude or another general AI assistant to a broker in order to use conversational AI for financial-market work.

That is one model. Chilla is another: the financial worker itself.

Tell Chilla what you are working towards. Chilla works towards your goals in the financial markets and reports back, within the boundaries you approve.
