Hosted automation + uptime

Do I need a VPS for automated trading?

It depends on where the automation runs. If the trading software lives on your own computer, something has to keep that software online. With hosted automation, your personal laptop does not have to stay awake just to keep the work running.

September 13, 20265 min read

VPS advice appears everywhere in automated trading because many traditional setups run the automation on the user’s own trading terminal.

If that terminal closes, the computer sleeps, the internet drops or the machine restarts, software that depends on that machine can stop seeing new conditions or carrying out its work.

A VPS solves that problem by giving the automation another computer that stays online. But a VPS is not a universal requirement for every kind of automated trading product.

Why do many trading bots need a VPS?

A self-hosted trading bot has to run somewhere.

If the bot runs inside software on your laptop or desktop, your machine becomes part of the automation setup. Keeping it available may mean leaving it powered on, preventing sleep, maintaining the internet connection and dealing with updates or restarts.

A VPS moves that responsibility to a remote computer. You still have to choose, configure and maintain the automation that runs there, but your personal computer no longer has to stay awake.

Does every automated trading product need a VPS?

No. Hosted automation runs on the service provider’s infrastructure rather than depending on the user’s personal computer to stay online.

That means “Do I need a VPS?” is really a question about the product’s operating model. A self-hosted bot may need one. A hosted product can remove that setup burden from the user.

Do I need a VPS for Chilla?

No. Chilla is hosted software.

Your phone or computer is where you talk to Chilla, review what matters, authorize work and inspect what happened. It is not the machine that has to stay powered on for active Chilla work to continue.

You do not need to rent a separate VPS, leave a trading terminal open on your laptop, or keep your personal computer awake just to keep an authorized Chilla task running.

Can I close Chilla after activating work?

Yes. Once the work is properly authorized and active, closing the app or browser does not by itself stop that work.

Chilla can continue carrying supported market work within the boundaries you approved and report back when you return.

Your device still matters when Chilla needs a new decision from you, when a sensitive account step requires your control, or when you want to change, pause, inspect or stop the work.

What happens if my home power or internet goes off?

Your personal power or internet interruption does not turn your laptop into the point of failure for already active hosted Chilla work.

You may temporarily be unable to open Chilla or respond to something that needs you. Active work itself does not depend on your device remaining online.

Hosted software can still have its own outages, and live work also depends on market data and external trading-account providers. No hosted service can truthfully promise that interruptions are impossible.

Does hosted mean I never have to check anything?

No. Hosted removes an infrastructure chore. It does not remove your authority or the need to understand the work you allow.

You still choose the material work when choices belong to you. You can come back to ask what happened, inspect Flow, change direction, pause or stop active work.

Chilla is designed so you do not have to babysit the runtime just to participate.

What if I want to run my own bot instead?

Then a VPS can still be a sensible tool, especially if your bot must remain connected whenever its conditions may occur.

That setup gives you direct control over the software and environment. It also leaves more of the operating work with you: choosing the bot, configuring it, keeping the environment healthy and deciding what logic it should run.

Chilla is aimed at a different relationship. You start with what you are working towards, keep control of the material choices, and let the product carry more of the ongoing machinery.

What should I ask before choosing an automation product?

  • Does the automation run on my device or as a hosted service?
  • Do I need to keep a trading terminal open?
  • Do I need to rent and maintain a VPS?
  • Do I have to bring my own strategy or bot?
  • What happens when my personal internet goes down?
  • Which decisions still belong to me?
  • Can I see what happened after I come back?

The short version

You need an always-available machine when the automation depends on software you are running yourself. A VPS is one common way to provide that machine.

Chilla is hosted software, so you do not need to make your laptop or a rented VPS responsible for keeping authorized Chilla work alive.

Chilla works towards your goals in the financial markets and reports back, within the boundaries you approve.
