How Chilla works

Chilla is a financial worker you can talk to. The experience starts with what you are trying to achieve, not with a dashboard full of trading machinery.

January 20, 2025Updated September 12, 20266 min read

Most financial software starts by asking you to operate the software: connect an account, choose settings, understand terminology, and keep checking whether everything is doing what you expected.

Chilla starts somewhere simpler. You say what you are working towards in ordinary language. Chilla handles the product complexity and brings the choices that still need you into the conversation.

Tell Chilla what you’re working towards.

1. Start with the goal

You might say, “I want to grow this account steadily without spending my day watching markets.” That starts a conversation. It is not automatic permission for Chilla to do anything it wants with a financial account.

Chilla can use the product and account context available to it to remove choices that are not actually available and explain the options that remain.

2. Make the choices that matter

When a material choice such as style, account or market still matters, Chilla brings that decision to you instead of silently choosing for you.

If you do not know where to start, Chilla can keep the choice small — for example, one or two straightforward compatible styles to try — and explain how they differ. You still choose what to run.

Chilla can simplify the choice without becoming the source of the choice.

3. Review the PLAN

Once the required material choices are settled, Chilla shows the PLAN: the concise receipt for the work you are considering.

You can review it before moving forward. Approving the PLAN takes you to the required disclosure and agreement for that work; Activate is the final step that puts it live.

4. Choose demo or live

You can try Chilla in demo or use a supported live provider account. Demo is an option, not a requirement just because you are new.

When live work needs an external account, your funds remain with the provider; Beaverly does not custody them.

For supported provider journeys, Chilla can help you navigate setup and account-management pages. Sensitive steps stay with you: passwords, OTP or MFA, identity checks, legal declarations, approvals, transaction details and money-moving confirmations.

Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.

5. Chilla works inside what you activated

After activation, Chilla can carry ongoing market work inside the boundaries you authorized without asking you to approve every individual market action.

If something materially changes and needs new authority, it comes back to you.

6. Chilla can decide not to act

A dependable worker should not look busy for the sake of looking useful. Sometimes the right outcome is to stay out because conditions do not fit the active work.

That is still part of the job. Chilla can tell you that nothing happened and explain the user-relevant reason.

7. Chilla reports back

You should not need to reconstruct the story from raw activity. Chilla can bring the important part back into the conversation in plain language.

When you want the activity trail, Flow remains available to inspect.

8. The rest of the product is there when you need it

Chat being primary does not mean everything else disappears. Accounts, Goals, Active Markets, Flow, Work Capacity, billing, settings and direct controls remain available as useful context and controls rather than a maze you must operate before Chilla can help.

Where M-II fits

M-II is Beaverly’s financial execution system underneath authorized live work. Chilla stays simple to use while live financial work remains bounded by what you authorized.

You talk to Chilla. M-II carries the live work.

The relationship in one line

Tell Chilla what you are working towards. Make the material choices that belong to you. Review and activate the PLAN. Chilla works within those boundaries and reports back.

You Chill. Chilla Works.
