How Chilla works

Chilla is a financial worker you can talk to. The experience starts with what you are trying to achieve, not with a dashboard full of trading machinery.

January 20, 2025Updated September 1, 20266 min read

Most financial software starts by asking you to operate the software: connect an account, choose settings, understand terminology, and keep checking whether everything is doing what you expected.

Chilla is being built around the opposite idea. You start by saying what you are working towards in ordinary language. Chilla does the work of turning that intent into something concrete enough for you to inspect and approve.

Tell Chilla what you’re working towards.

1. Start with the goal

You might say something like, “I want to grow this account steadily without spending my day watching markets.” That sentence is not an executable instruction by itself. It is the beginning of the conversation.

Chilla can use the supported product context available to it to prepare one or more bounded pieces of work: the style, compatible markets, comfort, account or environment, and the assumptions that matter.

The important part is that Chilla can reason about the work without silently giving itself authority to perform it.

2. Review what Chilla prepared

Before material work becomes active, you can see what Chilla is proposing and change it. Conversational edits and visible controls are two ways of changing the same underlying proposal, not two separate systems.

When the material configuration is ready, approval is bound to that exact version. If an important part changes later, the changed version needs to be reviewed and approved again.

Chilla can be intelligent. Intelligence is not authority.

3. Start in Chilla Demo if you want

You do not need to connect a live financial account just to understand the experience. Chilla Demo is Beaverly’s built-in synthetic environment, designed so you can see how Chilla behaves before connecting an external account.

Demo funds are not real funds and cannot be withdrawn. The point is to make the product understandable before asking you for a provider connection.

4. Live work stays separate from conversation

Once you authorize live work, the conversation is still not the execution engine. M-II is Beaverly’s engineered financial execution system underneath Chilla. It owns the deterministic work of carrying an authorized task through validation, provider communication, lifecycle control, and traceability.

That separation matters. A language model should not be able to say “the user approved this” and make it true. Approval, account ownership, sizing, execution state, and other financial truth come from the systems that own them.

Your funds remain in your account with your chosen account provider. Beaverly does not custody them.

5. Chilla can decide not to act

A dependable worker should not look busy for the sake of looking useful. Sometimes the correct outcome is to stay out because conditions do not fit the authorized way of working toward your goal.

That is still work. Chilla can report that nothing happened and why, instead of manufacturing activity just to look active.

6. Chilla reports back

The user should not need to reconstruct the story from raw events. Chilla can bring the important part back into the conversation in plain language, while Flow preserves the factual event trail underneath.

That means the answer can be simple while the source remains inspectable.

7. You can still open the machinery

Chat being primary does not mean everything else disappears. Accounts, Goals, Flow, Active Markets, usage, and direct controls can remain available as contextual surfaces. They simply stop being the first thing every user has to operate.

If you need to disconnect an account, stop active work, use a kill switch, inspect Flow, or check a canonical account state, those controls should stay deterministic and direct.

The relationship in one line

You say what you are working towards. Chilla turns that into bounded work you can inspect and approve. M-II handles authorized live execution. Flow keeps the factual trail. Chilla reports the important part back to you.

You Chill. Chilla Works.
