How Chilla works

Chilla is a financial worker you can talk to. Tell it what you are working towards, stay in control of what matters, then let Chilla carry the work and report back.

January 20, 2025Updated September 13, 20265 min read

Most financial software starts by asking you to operate the software: learn the terminology, configure the machinery, keep checking it, and know where to go when something changes.

Chilla starts somewhere simpler. You say what you are working towards in ordinary language. The conversation is the interface.

Tell Chilla what you’re working towards.

Start with the goal

You might say, “I want to grow this account steadily without spending my day watching markets.” Chilla can work from that goal instead of making you begin with charts, trading code or a long setup flow.

It uses the product and account context available to it to keep the conversation relevant and remove choices that are not actually available.

You stay in control of what matters

Chilla absorbs product complexity without quietly taking over the decisions that still belong to you.

When something material needs your choice, Chilla brings it into the conversation and can explain the supported options simply. Live work is shown to you before it goes live.

That boundary is meant to feel natural inside the conversation rather than like operating another dashboard.

Demo or live

You can use Chilla Demo or a supported live trading account. Demo is optional.

Chilla Demo follows real market prices while the balance and resulting activity are simulated inside Chilla. The simulated balance cannot be withdrawn and no live order is sent to an external provider.

With live work, your real funds remain with the provider hosting your supported trading account. Beaverly does not custody them, and provider execution conditions can affect what happens live.

Chilla carries the ongoing work

Once live work is active, Chilla can carry the ongoing market activity inside the boundaries you approved without asking you to manually approve every individual market action.

An active task does not mean constant activity. Sometimes Chilla stays out because conditions do not fit the way the work is meant to run.

Then it reports back

You should not need to reconstruct the story from a terminal or spend the day watching charts. Chilla brings the important part back into the conversation in plain language.

When you want the underlying activity trail, Flow remains available to inspect.

Provider setup can stay conversational too

For supported provider journeys, Chilla can use the web to help with trading-account setup and routine account-management navigation.

Sensitive steps stay with you, including passwords, OTP or MFA, identity checks, legal declarations, account approvals, transaction details and money-moving confirmations. Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.

The rest of Chilla is there when you need it

Conversation being primary does not mean useful controls disappear. Accounts, Goals, Active Markets, Flow, Work Capacity, billing and settings remain available when you want to inspect or control something directly.

Where M-II fits

M-II is Beaverly’s financial execution system underneath authorized live work.

You talk to Chilla. M-II carries the live work.

The relationship in one line

Tell Chilla what you are working towards. Stay in control of what matters. Chilla works within the boundaries you approve and reports back.

You Chill. Chilla Works.
