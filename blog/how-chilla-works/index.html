How Chilla Works

Discover how Chilla runs your approved boundaries in the background while you focus on life. Learn how the product keeps your setup clear, bounded, and visible.

January 20, 2025 6 min read Beverly

Chilla runs on M-II, Beaverly's automation infrastructure. It is designed to run your approved boundaries in the background while keeping your setup clear, bounded, and visible in Flow.

Getting Started

When you create a Chilla account and sign in:

  1. Connect your trading account – Securely link a supported account provider.
  2. Create a task – Choose your style and boundaries.
  3. Activate – Once you authorize, Chilla runs your task whether you're logged in or not.

Chilla works 24/7 in the background, running your task without emotional interference—until you pause or stop.

Your Tasks. Chilla's Automation.

Chilla isn't a traditional trading bot that makes decisions for you. It's an automation co-pilot that automates within your boundaries.

  • You decide: Pick your style, set your risk cap.
  • Chilla automates: Monitors conditions and acts within your comfort boundary without emotion or bias.
  • You stay in control: Pause, adjust, or stop anytime. Your funds stay in your trading account—always.

Chilla doesn't recommend strategies or promise outcomes. It simply automates what you instruct, faithfully and continuously, across multiple markets and providers (on paid tiers).

We share activity history for each style in-app—not as guarantees, but as transparency. Real results depend on your provider, market conditions, and the task you create.

How Chilla Automates Intelligently

Chilla is disciplined. It waits for alignment across context, structure, and micro validation within your chosen style.

Chilla follows your risk boundaries strictly. Losses happen—markets are unpredictable—but Chilla won't override your caps or chase hype. It automates within the rules you set, period.

Why Chilla is Different

Chilla is built as an automation infrastructure, not a decision-making platform:

  • You remain in control: All styles, markets, and risk caps are set by you.
  • Chilla automates with discipline: No emotional pressure, no bias, no FOMO.
  • Platform-agnostic: Connect supported providers. Your funds never leave your account.
  • 24/7 monitoring: Chilla doesn't sleep, so you can.
  • Transparent Flow updates: See what Chilla did and why, in plain language.

Just connect your provider, create a task, and step back. Review Flow updates over 3–6 months to see how Chilla maintains discipline in the background.

Chilla is built for the long gameletting you trade markets without the burnout. You just chill. Chilla works.
