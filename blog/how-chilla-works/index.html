How Chilla works

Chilla is a financial worker you can talk to. The experience starts with what you are trying to achieve, not with a dashboard full of trading machinery.

January 20, 2025Updated September 12, 20266 min read

Most financial software starts by asking you to operate the software: connect an account, choose settings, understand terminology, and keep checking whether everything is doing what you expected.

Chilla starts somewhere simpler. You say what you are working towards in ordinary language. Chilla can turn that goal into supported work you can understand before anything goes live.

Tell Chilla what you’re working towards.

1. Start with the goal

You might say, “I want to grow this account steadily without spending my day watching markets.” That starts a conversation. It is not automatic permission for Chilla to do anything it wants with a financial account.

Chilla can use the product context available to it to prepare supported choices such as a style, markets, Comfort, account context and other assumptions that matter to the work.

2. Review what matters

Before material live work becomes active, you can see the choices that matter and change them. You can ask questions, ask for another option, or edit the work until it matches what you intend to authorize.

Chilla can be intelligent. Intelligence is not unlimited authority.

3. Start in Chilla Demo if you want

You do not need to connect a live financial account just to understand the experience. Chilla Demo lets you see how Chilla works with a goal before connecting an external account.

Demo funds are not real funds and cannot be withdrawn.

4. Connect when live work needs it

When you are ready for live work, Chilla can work through a supported external provider account. Your funds remain with that provider; Beaverly does not custody them.

For supported provider journeys, Chilla can also help you navigate setup and account-management pages. Sensitive steps stay with you: passwords, OTP or MFA, identity checks, legal declarations, approvals, transaction details and money-moving confirmations.

Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.

5. Authorize the work

Once the material work looks right, you authorize it. Chilla can then carry ongoing market work inside the boundaries you approved without asking you to approve every individual market action.

If something materially changes and needs new authority, it comes back to you.

6. Chilla can decide not to act

A dependable worker should not look busy for the sake of looking useful. Sometimes the right outcome is to stay out because conditions do not fit the active work.

That is still part of the job. Chilla can tell you that nothing happened and explain the user-relevant reason.

7. Chilla reports back

You should not need to reconstruct the story from raw activity. Chilla can bring the important part back into the conversation in plain language.

When you want the underlying activity trail, Flow remains available to inspect.

8. The rest of the product is there when you need it

Chat being primary does not mean everything else disappears. Accounts, Goals, Active Markets, Flow, Work Capacity, billing, settings and direct controls remain available as useful context and controls rather than a maze you must operate before Chilla can help.

Where M-II fits

M-II is Beaverly’s financial execution system underneath authorized live work. It exists so Chilla can stay conversational while financial work remains bounded by what the user approved.

You talk to Chilla. M-II carries the live work.

The relationship in one line

You say what you are working towards. Chilla prepares work you can review and authorize. It works within those boundaries and reports back. Flow stays available when you want the activity trail.

You Chill. Chilla Works.
