Demo + live markets

Is demo trading real?

Chilla Demo uses real market prices and real Chilla trading actions. Its balance is synthetic and cannot be withdrawn. You can see Chilla work against the market without sending live orders to an external trading provider.

September 13, 20265 min read

“Demo” can sound like fake charts, random numbers or a toy version of a product. That is not what useful demo trading means.

Chilla Demo follows real market prices while using a synthetic balance. The market information and Chilla's trading actions are real; the balance and any gains are non-withdrawable Demo amounts.

What is real in Chilla Demo?

Chilla Demo follows real market prices, so you can watch Chilla actually work against the market instead of clicking around a fake tutorial.

That means the market can move for real while Chilla is working. You can see how the product behaves, what it reports back, and when it acts or stays out without putting real funds at risk.

What is synthetic?

The Demo balance is synthetic. It is not money held for you by Beaverly and it cannot be withdrawn. Chilla's trading actions are carried out and recorded inside Chilla Demo.

Chilla Demo carries out its trading actions inside Chilla. It does not send live orders to an external trading-account provider.

So is Chilla Demo a fake account?

Chilla Demo is a real Chilla account environment with a synthetic balance.

The useful distinction is simple: real market prices, real Chilla trading actions and a synthetic, non-withdrawable balance. Demo activity takes place inside Chilla rather than through an external trading account.

What changes when Chilla works live?

When you connect a real trading account, Chilla can carry approved work with real money there. Your funds stay with your provider.

Once a provider is involved, real execution conditions matter too. A provider can have its own prices, spreads, minimums, latency, order handling, rejection rules and other execution conditions. A demo cannot reproduce every provider-side condition perfectly because it is not actually sending the order there.

Do I have to use Demo before going live?

No. Chilla Demo is optional.

You can start by telling Chilla what you are working towards. When the work needs an account, use Chilla Demo or connect your trading account.

What is Demo actually good for?

  • Seeing how Chilla works without using real funds.
  • Understanding how Chilla reports activity back to you.
  • Watching how Chilla works with real market prices.
  • Learning the product without treating the synthetic balance as withdrawable money.
  • Comparing the Demo experience with live provider execution later, if you choose to go live.

The short answer

Chilla Demo uses real market prices and carries out real trading actions inside Chilla. Its balance is synthetic and cannot be withdrawn, and no live order is sent to an external provider.

When you're ready to use real money, connect your trading account. Your money stays with your provider, and Chilla works there within the boundaries you approve.
