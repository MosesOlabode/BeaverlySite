What Plug & Chill Isn't

Let's be clear about what this isn't:

❌ Not a Get-Rich-Quick Scheme

Chilla doesn't promise returns. Markets are unpredictable. Plug & chill is about consistent automation, not guaranteed outcomes.

❌ Not "Set and Forget"

You stay in control. You can pause, adjust, or stop anytime. Plug & chill means stepping back, not checking out.

❌ Not a Decision-Maker

Chilla doesn't pick strategies for you or tell you what's "best." You choose your style, and boundaries. Chilla runs your trading task within the boundary you chose—faithfully, consistently, 24/7.
