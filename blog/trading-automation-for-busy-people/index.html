Automation + busy lives

Can I automate trading while I’m at work?

Yes. The point of useful automation is that supported market work can continue without requiring you to spend your workday watching charts.

September 13, 20265 min read

Most people have another life to live. They are at work, in school, running a business, taking care of family or simply doing something more useful than staring at a chart for hours.

That makes financial markets awkward for anyone who thinks participation requires constant attention. Manual trading often does. Signals can still leave the execution to you. Alerts can tell you that something happened and still arrive at the worst possible moment.

Automation changes the time problem by letting software carry supported work after you have made the decisions that actually belong to you.

Can software really keep working while I am busy?

Yes. That is one of the basic reasons trading automation exists.

With Chilla, you start by telling it what you are working towards. Chilla handles the product complexity and brings the choices that still need you into the conversation. You decide what should run and see the live work before it starts.

Once that work is live, Chilla can carry ongoing supported market work inside those boundaries and report back. You do not need to approve every individual market action.

Do I need a VPS or keep my computer on?

No. Chilla is hosted software. Your laptop or phone is where you talk to Chilla and check the work; it is not the machine that has to stay powered on for active Chilla work to continue.

You do not need to rent a VPS, leave a trading terminal open all day, or keep your personal computer awake just to keep Chilla running. You can close the app and come back later.

Your device still matters when Chilla needs your input or when you want to inspect or change something. And like any hosted service, Chilla can still be affected by Beaverly, network, market-data or provider availability. Hosted does not mean failure is impossible.

Does that mean I can completely forget about it?

No. Delegation should reduce the operating burden without removing your control.

You should still understand the work you allow, where your money stays, the boundaries you approved and how to pause or stop the work. Material changes that need you come back to you.

Chilla is designed to keep you out of repetitive market monitoring while keeping you involved when your decision is actually required.

What happens if there is nothing worth doing?

Then the useful action can be no action.

Automation should not manufacture activity simply to look busy. An active Chilla task can wait when conditions do not fit the way the authorized work is meant to run.

You may come back from work and receive a report saying Chilla stayed out. That can be a perfectly normal result of the work continuing as authorized.

Do I have to become a trader first?

No. Chilla is built so you can start from a goal in ordinary language rather than from charts, indicators or trading code.

When a style, trading account or market choice matters, Chilla can explain the supported options simply. You make the material choice. The software carries the machinery around it.

If you already understand markets, that knowledge can still help you ask better questions. It is not a requirement to spend your day operating the product.

Where does my money stay?

For live work, your funds remain with the provider hosting your supported trading account. Beaverly does not hold those funds.

Connecting a trading account is also separate from starting live Chilla work. A connection alone does not give Chilla unlimited authority over the account.

What if I still need help setting up the trading account?

For supported provider journeys, Chilla can use the web to help with trading-account setup and routine account-management tasks.

Sensitive steps remain yours: passwords, OTP or MFA, identity checks, legal declarations, account approvals, transaction details and money-moving confirmations.

The goal is to remove unnecessary setup work without pretending sensitive decisions should disappear.

How do I know what happened while I was away?

Chilla reports back in conversation. If you want the underlying activity record, Flow is there to inspect what happened.

That means the product relationship is not “turn on a black box and hope.” You can leave the screen, come back later and see what matters without reconstructing the entire day yourself.

Is this guaranteed to make money while I work?

No. Automation solves an operating problem, not market uncertainty.

Financial markets involve risk and losses are possible. Chilla does not guarantee profit, remove market risk or decide that market participation is appropriate for your personal circumstances.

The short version

If your job or life makes constant market monitoring unrealistic, automation can carry supported financial-market work while you are busy. With Chilla, you set the goal, stay in control of what matters, then let Chilla operate within those boundaries and report back. Your personal computer does not have to stay on to keep the work running.

Chilla works towards your goals in the financial markets and reports back.
