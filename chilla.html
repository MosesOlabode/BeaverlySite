How Chilla works

One conversation. Ongoing work.

You don’t build a workflow before Chilla can help. Start with what you’re working towards. The rest of the relationship stays conversational.

01

Tell Chilla the goal.

Say what you’re working towards in your own words. Chilla starts from the goal, not from a setup screen.

02

Chilla gets to work.

It works toward the goal in the financial markets and keeps going without needing you to sit there managing every step.

03

Ask. Change. Check.

Come back to ask what happened, change direction, check progress, or understand why Chilla did — or didn’t do — something.
