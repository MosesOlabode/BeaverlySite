How Chilla works

One conversation. Ongoing work.

Start with your financial goal. Chilla works toward it in the markets, carries the useful work around the goal and keeps you involved where your decision matters. Underneath the simple conversation, Chilla AI is built to carry ongoing work, not just answer questions.

Demo or live

Try the work before real money, or connect when you’re ready.

Chilla Demo follows real market prices and tracks the work internally without sending live orders to a provider. Its balance is simulated and cannot be withdrawn. Live work uses your real balance with a supported provider.

Your money stays with your provider when you go live. Beaverly does not hold your funds.
