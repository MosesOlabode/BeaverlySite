Mission

Move financial work from something people operate to something software can carry.

Not by hiding important decisions. By making the machinery easier to direct, easier to understand, and dependable enough to keep working when the user steps away.

Founder

Built from inside the problem.

Beaverly was founded by Moses Olabode after years working directly in financial markets. Chilla grew from a practical question: how much of the repetitive work around financial goals could dependable software take over without taking control away from the person using it?

The answer became a company, Chilla, and the execution infrastructure underneath it.

Founding partner

Security was part of the build from the start.

Kolawole Oyedeji brings 11 years in cybersecurity across banking, fintech, telco and product security. At Beaverly, he leads security across the product and infrastructure Chilla and M-II depend on.

For software trusted with live financial work, security is part of how the product is built — not a layer added later.
