What that means

It can do the provider work, not just tell you what to click.

Chilla can open supported provider websites, move through routine screens, find account-management areas and work through ordinary setup steps. When a page needs your judgment or a sensitive action, Chilla brings you in.

Open

Go to the supported provider page the task needs.

Navigate

Move through ordinary setup and account-management screens.

Explain

Tell you what matters when a page needs your attention.

Your device

It does not need control of your laptop or phone.

Your device is where you talk to Chilla, see what it is doing and take over when needed. Chilla’s own computer handles the supported website work.

Boundaries

The sensitive steps stay yours.

01

Passwords & verification

You enter passwords, OTP/MFA codes and other credential information yourself. When you take over a sensitive credential step, Chilla does not observe what you enter.

02

Identity & legal steps

Identity checks, KYC information, legal declarations and provider approvals remain your steps.

03

Money movement

Chilla can help you reach supported deposit, withdrawal or transfer areas. You choose the transaction details and make the consequential confirmation.

04

Routine work

Supported navigation and ordinary provider steps can stay with Chilla so you do not have to operate every screen yourself.

Take over and hand back

When a step needs you, Chilla gives you the screen.

Finish the part that belongs to you, then Chilla can continue the supported journey. You do not have to turn the whole provider process into a list of instructions for yourself.

Financial authority

Using a computer is not permission to move your money.

Computer use does not authorize live financial work or give Chilla unlimited authority over a trading account. Connecting an account, reviewing the PLAN and activating live work remain separate user-controlled steps.
