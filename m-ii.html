Why it exists

Conversation should not mean unlimited financial authority.

Chilla needs to feel natural to talk to without making live financial work casual. M-II exists to keep those responsibilities separate.

Chilla

Understands what you’re working towards, talks with you, checks in, and reports back.

M-II

Carries authorized live financial work underneath the experience.

Boundaries

Authorized work stays authorized work.

M-II is built around the work the user has approved. Chilla can stay useful and ongoing without treating a conversation as permission to expand that work on its own.

Your goalYour authorizationLive workReport back

Restraint

Doing nothing can be part of doing the job.

Live financial software should not manufacture activity just to look busy. When work should not proceed, the right outcome can be to stay out and report back.
