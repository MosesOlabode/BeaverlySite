Automation capacity

How automation capacity works

Every day, Chilla has a capacity for how much automation work it can run on your behalf. Think of it as the total automation fuel available to your account in a 24-hour window.

It resets daily.

Capacity refreshes each day, so you're never penalised for yesterday's activity.

Different tasks use different amounts.

Some automation runs are lighter; others — particularly when monitoring many markets simultaneously — use more capacity. Chilla manages this automatically while your task remains active.

Guardrails protect system reliability.

Paid plans are designed to feel roomy in normal use. The guardrails exist to keep Chilla reliable and consistent for everyone — not to restrict everyday automation.

Questions

Good questions, calm answers.

What limits apply to each tier?

Chill Architect includes limited weekly automation capacity. Chill Mode includes daily automation capacity and 1 connected account. Deep Chill includes 5x more daily automation capacity and up to 5 connected accounts. Peak Chill includes the highest daily automation capacity and up to 12 connected accounts. Guardrails still protect reliability for everyone.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — you can upgrade or downgrade anytime from inside Chilla. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. If you'd like to talk through a mid-cycle change, reach out to the team at hello@beaverlyai.com.

Does Chilla hold my funds?

No — never. Your funds remain in your own trading account at all times. Beaverly doesn't hold, manage, or have access to your capital. We provide automation that follows the boundaries you authorize. You withdraw, deposit, and manage your money directly through your own account.

What is automation capacity?

It's the total amount of automation work Chilla can run on your behalf in a single day. Think of it like daily automation fuel — it powers the monitoring, boundary checks and activity happening in the background. It resets every 24 hours, and you can always see where you stand inside Chilla.

Why do I see values like 10,000× in-app?

The × suffix represents individual automation cycles — each one a discrete piece of work Chilla completes while your task remains active. 10,000× means 10,000 automation runs available that day. Most users with active tasks find their daily allocation more than comfortable.

Do different styles use capacity differently?

Yes. Lighter monitoring actions use less capacity; more complex tasks, particularly those watching many markets simultaneously — use a bit more. Chilla manages this automatically. Your usage panel always shows how much you've used and how much remains, so there's no guesswork.

What counts as an account connection?

An account connection is a link between Chilla and a single trading account. If you have one account, you need one connection. If you want Chilla to automate across multiple accounts — different accounts, regions, or account types — each one counts as a separate account connection.

Can I try Chilla live before upgrading?

Yes. Chill Architect is free and lets you connect your account, choose styles, set comfort levels, and experience Chilla live within limited weekly automation capacity. Flow can show the live activity Chilla performs while it uses that capacity.

Which plan if I want more than one account?

Deep Chill supports up to 5 account connections — a comfortable starting point for multi-account automation. If you need more, Peak Chill supports up to 12 connected trading accounts.

Do all plans include Flow?

Yes. Flow — your real-time decision log — is included across all plans, including the free Chill Architect tier. Transparency is core to how Chilla works. You'll always be able to see what Chilla is doing and why, inside your active tasks.

Does Chilla promise outcomes?

No — and we'll never claim otherwise. Chilla is calm automation, not a financial advisor or performance engine. All markets involve uncertainty. What Chilla offers is transparency, clear boundaries, and the removal of emotional or time-based friction from trade activity. Outcomes depend on market conditions and the styles you choose.

Is yearly billing locked in?

Yearly plans are billed as a single annual payment and include a meaningful saving compared to monthly billing. If your circumstances change, reach out to the team at support@beaverlyai.com — we're here to help.
