How it works

Your account. Your challenge. A plan made for both.

01

Connect your prop account

Connect the account to Chilla through cTrader. Chilla uses the connected account details to understand the account it will be working with.

02

Chilla checks the challenge

Chilla looks at the challenge target, drawdown limits, trading rules and the details that affect how the account can be traded.

03

Chilla builds your plan

Chilla puts together a plan for your account and its rules. It chooses the approaches that fit the challenge and current conditions. The plan can use one or more approaches.

04

You review and approve

Chilla shows you the proposed work and the limits it will follow. You review it and approve it before the trading work starts.

05

Chilla trades and tracks progress

Chilla carries out the approved work, watches the challenge progress and account limits, and reports back as it goes.

After you qualify

Chilla can keep managing the funded account.

Once you qualify, Chilla can continue the approved work on your funded account. It keeps tracking the account and reports back so you can follow what is happening.

Trading

Chilla carries out the work you approved.

Account limits

Chilla keeps the account rules and limits in view.

Updates

Chilla tracks progress and reports back.

You stay in control

You approve the plan before Chilla starts.

Connecting your account gives Chilla the account context it needs. You still review the proposed work and approve it before live trading begins. Your funds stay with the provider that hosts your account.
