1. What the service is

Beaverly Innovative Systems Ltd. provides software that includes Chilla, a user-facing financial worker, and M-II, the financial execution system underneath authorized live work.

Beaverly is not a broker, bank, custodian, licensed investment adviser, or portfolio manager. The Services are non-custodial and non-advisory. Live financial activity occurs through a third-party financial account that you choose and authorize.

2. Market risk and possible financial loss

Participation in financial markets can result in partial or total loss of capital. Prices can move sharply, liquidity can change, spreads can widen, leverage can amplify gains and losses, and market conditions can behave differently from historical periods.

You should consider your financial circumstances, tolerance for loss, and ability to bear losses before authorizing live work. Beaverly does not determine whether market participation is suitable for you.

3. No investment advice, recommendation, or guaranteed outcome

Beaverly does not provide personalized investment advice, portfolio management, or recommendations to buy, sell, hold, or allocate capital to a particular financial instrument.

Supported styles, markets, product explanations, generated conversation, Flow reports, demo results, and historical activity are product information, not promises of profit or future performance. No representation is made that any user will achieve returns, avoid losses, or reproduce past activity.

Chilla can prepare work for your review. Preparation is not a guarantee, recommendation, or authorization. You decide whether to approve a material configuration and whether to keep it active.

4. User-authorized, bounded financial work

Live work begins from an authorization you give to a specific material configuration presented by the Services. Once active, the authorized task may monitor market conditions and take supported market actions without asking you to approve every individual action.

The timing and size of an individual action may be determined by the approved style, market conditions, canonical sizing or comfort controls, account-provider rules, and other product safeguards. That does not give Chilla authority to change the material scope you approved without a new validated revision and approval where required.

You remain responsible for deciding whether to authorize, pause, stop, revise, or discontinue live work and how much capital you make available in the connected account.

5. Technology and execution risks

Automated financial systems depend on software, networks, market data, providers, and infrastructure. Risks include:

  • network latency, disconnection, outages, or delayed provider acknowledgements;
  • stale, delayed, malformed, or unavailable market data;
  • provider restrictions, maintenance, outages, rejected orders, pricing differences, slippage, or execution delays;
  • software defects, hardware failures, dependency failures, recovery delays, or unexpected interactions between systems;
  • rapid or unusual market conditions that make expected behavior difficult or impossible.

Beaverly designs the Services to fail safely where possible, but does not promise uninterrupted operation, error-free execution, or perfect third-party data.

6. Comfort, safeguards, and limits

Chilla may enforce product safeguards such as comfort boundaries, account/provider minimums, authorization checks, or other controls intended to constrain how authorized work can operate.

These controls are technical safeguards. They do not guarantee that a loss will stay below a particular amount in every market or provider condition. Price gaps, slippage, provider behavior, connectivity issues, or exceptional market movement can affect realized outcomes.

Where a control prevents activity, that does not mean a market outcome would otherwise have been profitable or unprofitable.

Live work depends on third parties, including financial account providers, market-data sources, payment providers, and infrastructure services. Beaverly does not control their rules, pricing, account restrictions, execution quality, availability, or regulatory obligations.

Compatibility can vary by region, account type, provider environment, market availability, and third-party policy. A provider can change or withdraw access independently of Beaverly.

9. No custody of funds

Beaverly does not hold, receive, deposit, withdraw, or custody money in your connected financial account. Funds remain with the account provider you selected. Beaverly charges separately for access to its software and service capacity.

10. Chilla Demo is simulated

Chilla Demo uses synthetic funds. Demo balances cannot be withdrawn and do not represent funds held by Beaverly. Demo activity can differ materially from live activity because live accounts introduce provider execution, slippage, liquidity, account rules, connectivity, and other real-world conditions.

Good or poor results in Chilla Demo are not a prediction of future live results.
