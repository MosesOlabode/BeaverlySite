Security at Beaverly

Simple boundaries that matter.

Chilla works with financial accounts. Beaverly keeps the important lines clear: your funds stay with your provider, live work needs your authorization, and sensitive provider steps stay under your control.

Non-custodial

Your money stays where it is.

Beaverly does not custody user funds. When Chilla works live, it does so through an account you authorize. Your capital remains with the account provider.

Funds

Remain with your external account provider.

Live work

Runs only after the material work has been authorized.

Control

You can return to Chilla to check, change, pause or stop active work.

Provider assistance

Chilla can help. Sensitive actions stay yours.

01

Navigation

Chilla can help navigate supported provider setup and account-management journeys.

02

Credentials

Passwords, OTP/MFA and other credential steps belong to you. When you take over a sensitive credential step, Chilla does not observe that entry activity.

03

Identity and declarations

Identity/KYC information, legal declarations and approvals remain user-owned steps.

04

Money movement

Chilla can help you reach supported deposit, withdrawal, transfer or settings areas. Transaction details and consequential confirmations remain yours.

Credentials & sessions

Your broker password is not Chilla’s data.

Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords. Where a provider session is retained under product policy so assistance can continue later, that session is protected with encryption at rest and in transit and kept isolated.
