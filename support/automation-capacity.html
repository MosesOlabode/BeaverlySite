Automation Capacity

Understanding your available capacity

Capacity shows how much room Chilla still has to work within your current capacity window. Chilla presents that room as a percentage rather than exposing internal metering units.

What capacity means

Capacity is the room available for Chilla to keep doing automated work for your active tasks. Beaverly measures several kinds of work underneath, but you do not need to manage those internal units.

  • 100% available means the full capacity for the current window is still available.
  • As Chilla works, the available percentage decreases.
  • When the capacity window resets, the available percentage returns for the new window.
  • Capacity is workload room. It is not a score, outcome estimate, or market signal.

What you will see in Chilla

  • Your capacity available as a percentage.
  • Your current capacity tier.
  • The relevant reset window for that tier.
  • Your connected-account allowance and other tier limits where relevant.
Simple by design: Chilla shows the percentage you can act on. Internal capacity counters stay internal.

Capacity tiers

Higher Chilla tiers provide more room for automated work and, on higher tiers, more connected live accounts.

  • Chill Architect: limited weekly capacity.
  • Chill Mode: standard daily capacity.
  • Deep Chill: expanded daily capacity.
  • Peak Chill: the highest daily capacity tier.

See the current prices and account allowances on the Pricing page.

Why capacity use can differ

Different tasks can require different amounts of work. Chilla handles that measurement automatically.

  • Some styles monitor more frequently than others.
  • Watching more markets can create more automated work.
  • Periods with more market activity can create more work than quiet periods.
  • Multiple connected accounts can increase the amount of work Chilla is doing.

When capacity gets low

Chilla can surface a warning when available capacity is getting low and can stop starting new work when the current capacity window is exhausted. Existing safety and task boundaries remain in force.

If your normal workload regularly needs more room, you can compare higher capacity tiers from Pricing.

Capacity FAQ

Why don't I see the old large capacity numbers anymore?

Those values are internal metering details. Chilla now presents capacity as a percentage so you can understand available room without learning Beaverly's internal accounting units.

Does lower available capacity mean worse outcomes?

No. Capacity only describes how much automation room remains in the current window.

Do all tiers reset the same way?

No. Chill Architect uses a limited weekly capacity model, while paid tiers use daily capacity. Chilla shows the relevant window for your tier.
