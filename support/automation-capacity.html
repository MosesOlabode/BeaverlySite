What capacity means

Capacity is the room Chilla has available to keep working. Chilla shows your available capacity as a percentage.

  • 100% available means your full capacity for the current window is available.
  • As Chilla works, the available percentage decreases.
  • Capacity resets according to your tier.
  • Capacity is not an outcome estimate or market signal.

Capacity tiers

Your tier determines how much work capacity Chilla has and how many live accounts it can work on.

  • Chill Architect: Limited capacity.
  • Chill Mode: Standard capacity.
  • Deep Chill: Expanded capacity.
  • Peak Chill: Highest capacity.

See current prices and live-account allowances on Pricing.

When capacity gets low

Chilla lets you know when available capacity is getting low. If you regularly need more room, you can compare higher capacity tiers from Pricing.

Capacity FAQ

Does lower available capacity mean worse outcomes?

No. Capacity only shows how much work capacity remains in the current window.

When does capacity reset?

Chilla shows the reset window that applies to your tier.
