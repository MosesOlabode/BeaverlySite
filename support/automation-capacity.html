What Work Capacity means

Work Capacity is the room Chilla has available to keep working under your current tier. Chilla shows it in a simple user-facing form such as an available percentage.

  • More available capacity means more room for supported active work.
  • Current work can use part of that room.
  • New work can require additional capacity.
  • Capacity resets according to the rules of the current tier.
  • Capacity is not an outcome estimate, market signal, or measure of expected profit.

Before you activate new work

When capacity matters to a proposed task, Chilla can explain how much room current work is already using and whether the new work is expected to fit.

The preview is there to help you understand the effect of adding the work. It is an estimate, not a guarantee that every future day will use exactly the same amount of capacity.

Capacity tiers

Your tier determines the amount of Work Capacity available and how many live accounts Chilla can work on.

  • Chill Architect: Limited capacity.
  • Chill Mode: Standard capacity.
  • Deep Chill: Expanded capacity.
  • Peak Chill: Highest capacity.

See current prices and live-account allowances on Pricing.

When capacity gets low

Chilla can tell you when available capacity is becoming a constraint. You can reduce active work, wait for the applicable reset, or compare a tier with more room.

Work Capacity FAQ

Does lower capacity mean worse market outcomes?

No. Work Capacity only describes how much product capacity remains for active work.

Is the preview an exact forecast?

No. It is a practical estimate of expected capacity use based on the work being considered, not a guarantee of identical future consumption.

When does capacity reset?

Chilla shows the reset window that applies to your current tier.
