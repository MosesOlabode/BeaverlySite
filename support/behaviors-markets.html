What a style is

A style is a supported way Chilla can work in the financial markets. It describes the kind of market conditions and rhythm that a piece of work is built around.

Styles are not user-facing promises of returns. They are product capabilities Chilla can use when they fit the goal, supported market context and approved boundaries.

You do not have to choose first

In Chilla 3.0, the normal starting point is the goal. Chilla can prepare a supported style, market or market set, Comfort and other material choices from that conversation.

You can inspect those choices, ask why they fit, compare alternatives, or change them before authorization.

What a market means

A market is the financial market context where supported work can run. Availability depends on the connected provider/account, the style, the product's current support and account eligibility.

If a market is not supported for the selected work or account, Chilla should not treat it as available simply because it was mentioned in conversation.

How to read style information

When you open more detail about a style, you may see information designed to explain its historical temperament and expected rhythm.

  • Summary: what the style is designed to watch for.
  • How it acts: a plain-language description of its general rhythm.
  • Typical activity: whether the style has historically been quieter, occasional or more active.
  • Holding style: whether its historical activity was usually shorter, longer or mixed.
  • Compatible markets: where the style can currently be used with supported account context.
  • Activity history: deeper historical context when available.

Historical information is context, not a prediction of future results.

Temperament Review

Temperament Review is there to help you understand how a style has behaved in study history. Depending on the style and available history, it can include information about activity rhythm, quiet periods, movement sensitivity, consistency and market-by-market differences.

Use it to understand what the style may feel like to live with. Do not treat it as a guarantee, a safety score or a forecast of future profitability.

Can I ask Chilla about a style or market?

Yes. You can ask why Chilla prepared a particular style or market, what alternatives are supported, what the historical temperament looks like, or whether changing the market would materially change the proposed work.

Chilla should explain product information without turning the conversation into personalized financial advice.

Before authorization

  • Check the style and market choices if they matter to you.
  • Check the connected account that live work will use.
  • Review Comfort and any other material boundary.
  • Ask Chilla to change anything you do not want before authorizing the work.
