Ways Chilla Works & Markets

Different goals can call for different ways of working.

You do not need to learn a catalogue first. Tell Chilla what you want and it can surface available ways whose supplied characteristics match what you asked for.

What changes between the ways Chilla can work

Different ways can have different activity rhythms, holding character and market scope. Public names such as Quiet Sniper or Gilded Tide help you recognize a way of working; you do not need to learn an internal category system.

When you do not know which way to use

Just describe the goal or the kind of work you want. Chilla can search the available compatible ways by the characteristics you actually stated and surface a small editable starting set.

You can ask why a way was surfaced, compare the supplied differences, choose another one, or change the Plan before activation.

What Chilla can explain

When useful information is available, Chilla can explain a behaviour in practical terms such as:

  • what kind of activity it is designed around;
  • whether it has historically been quieter or more active;
  • whether its activity has generally been shorter, longer or mixed;
  • which supported markets it can currently use;
  • relevant historical behaviour when you ask for more context.

Historical behaviour is context, not a forecast of future results.

What a market means

A market is the financial market where supported work can run. Availability depends on the behaviour, trading account and current product support.

You do not need a market lesson before you can start. Chilla can explain a market when you ask, or when choosing one becomes the next material decision needed for your work.

Behaviour and market choices remain yours

Compatibility can narrow what is possible, but it does not silently choose the material work for you. When a behaviour, trading account or market still needs a decision, Chilla asks for that decision before the PLAN is created.

You can choose more than one supported item where the product allows it. Chilla keeps the choices you have already made as the conversation progresses.

Active Markets is different

Active Markets shows the market activity connected to work Chilla is already carrying or has completed. It is there for context and inspection; it is not a catalog you must study before starting.

Ask Chilla when you want the short explanation. Use Active Markets or ask Chilla to report back when you want more detail about the activity.

Before the PLAN

  • Choose the behaviour you want to try when Chilla asks.
  • Choose the trading account and market when they are material to the work.
  • Ask Chilla to explain the difference between the available choices if you are unsure.
  • Once the required material choices are settled, review the exact PLAN before moving to activation.
