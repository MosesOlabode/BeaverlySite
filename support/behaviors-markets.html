Styles and Markets

You do not need to learn the catalog first.

Start with what you are working towards. When a style or market choice matters, Chilla can narrow what is actually available and explain the smallest useful set in ordinary language. You choose what to run.

What a style is

A style is a supported way Chilla can work in the financial markets. Different styles can have different activity rhythms, holding behaviour and market support.

A style is not a promise of returns or a personalized recommendation.

When you do not know which style to pick

You can simply say so. Chilla can remove incompatible options, then put one or two straightforward supported styles in front of you to try and explain how they behave.

The shortlist is there to make the product easier to use. It is not Chilla deciding which financial choice is best for you. You choose the style you want to try.

What Chilla can explain

When useful information is available, Chilla can explain a style in practical terms such as:

  • what kind of activity it is designed around;
  • whether it has historically been quieter or more active;
  • whether its activity has generally been shorter, longer or mixed;
  • which supported markets it can currently use;
  • relevant historical behaviour when you ask for more context.

Historical behaviour is context, not a forecast of future results.

What a market means

A market is the financial market where supported work can run. Availability depends on the style, provider account and current product support.

You do not need a market lesson before you can start. Chilla can explain a market when you ask, or when choosing one becomes the next material decision needed for your work.

Style and market choices remain yours

Compatibility can narrow what is possible, but it does not silently choose the material work for you. When a style, account or market still needs a decision, Chilla asks for that decision before the PLAN is created.

You can choose more than one supported item where the product allows it. Chilla keeps the choices you have already made as the conversation progresses.

Active Markets is different

Active Markets shows the market activity connected to work Chilla is already carrying or has completed. It is there for context and inspection; it is not a catalog you must study before starting.

Ask Chilla when you want the short explanation. Use Active Markets or Flow when you want to inspect the activity itself.

Before the PLAN

  • Choose the style you want to try when Chilla asks.
  • Choose the account and market when they are material to the work.
  • Ask Chilla to explain the difference between the available choices if you are unsure.
  • Once the required material choices are settled, review the exact PLAN before moving to activation.
