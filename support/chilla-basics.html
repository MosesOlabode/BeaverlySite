Chilla Basics

Conversation is the main interface.

Chilla starts with what you are working towards. It handles the machinery, brings the material choices that belong to you into the conversation, carries authorized work, and reports back.

The basic relationship

Tell Chilla the goal

Use ordinary language. You do not need to translate your goal into a trading workflow before Chilla can help.

Make the choices that matter

Chilla narrows what is actually available and explains the small material choices that still belong to you.

Review and activate

Once the required choices are settled, review the PLAN, continue through the required disclosure, then activate the work when you are ready.

Chilla works and reports back

Ask what happened, check progress, change direction, or understand why Chilla acted or stayed out.

Useful context without dashboard hunting

Chilla can use relevant product context when it answers you, so you do not have to copy basic account, goal, market or Flow information into every conversation.

When you want to inspect those areas yourself, Accounts, Active Markets, Flow and Goals remain available from the Chilla interface.

Choices and the PLAN

Style, account and market choices remain yours when they are material to the work. Chilla can simplify the decision, explain the options and remove choices that are not actually available.

After the required choices are settled, the PLAN shows the material work you are considering. Approving the PLAN moves you to the required disclosure; activation is the final step that puts that reviewed work live.

Provider assistance

For supported provider journeys, Chilla can help you navigate setup or account-management pages. Sensitive actions remain yours.

  • Passwords and OTP/MFA stay with you.
  • Identity/KYC and legal declarations stay with you.
  • OAuth approval stays with you.
  • Transaction details and money-moving confirmations stay with you.
  • Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.

See Connected Accounts for details.

Common questions

Do I need to connect an account before I can talk to Chilla?

No. Start with the goal. When the work needs an account, you can choose demo or connect a supported live provider account.

Do I have to approve every market action?

No. Once work is activated, individual actions can happen inside that authorized scope without repeatedly asking for the same approval.

Can Chilla expand the work on its own?

A material change that needs new authority comes back to you for review.

Where do I see what happened?

Ask Chilla in conversation. Open Flow when you want the activity trail, or Active Markets when you want to inspect current and completed market activity.
