Chilla Basics

Conversation is the main interface.

Chilla 3.0 starts with what you are working towards. Most of the product exists to help that conversation understand your context, bring material choices to you, carry authorized work, and report back.

The basic relationship

Tell Chilla the goal

Use ordinary language. You do not need to translate your goal into a trading workflow before Chilla can help.

Review what matters

When material live work needs your authorization, Chilla presents the choices that need your attention.

Let Chilla work

After authorization, Chilla can carry ongoing work inside the boundaries you approved.

Come back and ask

Ask what happened, check progress, change direction, or understand why Chilla acted or stayed out.

Context without dashboard hunting

Chilla can use relevant product context when it answers you, so you do not have to manually copy basic account, goal, market or Flow information into every conversation.

When you want to inspect those areas yourself, they remain available from the Chilla interface.

Authorization

Talking to Chilla is not the same as authorizing live financial work. Chilla can discuss a goal and prepare work before anything is live.

When the material configuration needs your approval, you can inspect it, change it and authorize it. Chilla then works inside those approved boundaries.

Provider assistance

For supported provider journeys, Chilla can also help you navigate setup or account-management pages. Sensitive actions remain yours.

  • Passwords and OTP/MFA stay with you.
  • Identity/KYC and legal declarations stay with you.
  • OAuth approval stays with you.
  • Transaction details and money-moving confirmations stay with you.
  • Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.

See Connected Accounts for details.

Common questions

Do I need to connect an account before I can use Chilla?

No. You can start in Chilla Demo and begin with the goal.

Do I have to approve every market action?

No. You authorize the bounded work. Individual actions can then happen inside that scope without repeatedly asking for the same approval.

Can Chilla expand the work on its own?

Material changes that need new authority return to you for review.

Where do I see what happened?

Ask Chilla in conversation. Open Flow when you want the underlying activity trail.
