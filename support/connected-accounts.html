Connected Accounts

Your provider account stays yours.

Chilla can work through supported provider accounts and help with supported setup or account-management journeys. Beaverly does not hold your funds, and sensitive provider actions stay under your control.

What a connected account means

A connected account is an external financial account you authorize Chilla to work through. Your money remains with that provider. Beaverly is the software layer, not the provider, bank or custodian.

Supported providers and connection methods can change as integrations evolve. Use the Compatibility page for the current public list.

Start from the conversation

You do not need to connect an account before Chilla can understand what you are working towards. You can begin in Chilla Demo, discuss the goal, and connect a supported provider when live work actually needs it.

When a connection or provider step is needed, Chilla can guide you from the conversation rather than requiring you to learn an old dashboard workflow first.

Where Chilla can help on provider pages

For supported provider journeys, Chilla can help navigate setup and account-management pages and get you to the right place.

Chilla can help

Ordinary navigation, supported setup guidance, finding account-management areas, and explaining what you are looking at.

You take over

Passwords, OTP/MFA, identity/KYC information, legal declarations, OAuth approval, transaction details and consequential financial confirmations.

Your credentials stay private

  • Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.
  • When you take over a sensitive credential step, Chilla does not observe that credential-entry activity.
  • If a provider session is retained under product policy so assistance can continue later, that session is protected with encryption at rest and in transit and kept isolated.
  • You should never paste a broker password, OTP or recovery code into the Chilla conversation.

Deposits, withdrawals and transfers

Chilla may help you navigate to a supported provider’s deposit, withdrawal, transfer or settings area. That navigation does not give Chilla permission to decide or complete a money-moving action for you.

  • You choose the amount and relevant account or payment details.
  • You review provider terms, fees, conversion rates and verification requirements.
  • You make the consequential confirmation yourself.
  • The provider remains responsible for its own deposit and withdrawal processing.

See Deposits and Withdrawals for focused guidance.

Before live work

  • Confirm that the connected account is the one you intend Chilla to use.
  • Review the material work Chilla prepares before authorization.
  • Check that the provider account itself is ready for the markets or products involved.
  • Remember that connecting an account does not authorize unlimited activity.

Connected account FAQ

Does Beaverly hold my funds?

No. Funds remain with your external provider.

Does Chilla need to know my broker password?

No. Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords. Sensitive credential entry stays with you.

Can Chilla complete a withdrawal for me?

No. Chilla can help you navigate supported provider pages, while transaction details and consequential money-moving confirmations remain yours.

Can I start before connecting an account?

Yes. Chilla Demo lets you begin with the goal before you connect an external account.

Which providers are supported?

Check the Compatibility page for the current public list.
