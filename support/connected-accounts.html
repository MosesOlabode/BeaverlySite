Before you connect

  • Have access to the target connected account and any required provider verification flow.
  • Confirm whether you want a direct Deriv account or a cTrader account through a broker that supports cTrader.
  • Complete any required account-level verification steps before expecting funding, withdrawals, or automation to be available.
  • Check plan limits for number of account connections.

What a provider account means

A provider account is the trading account you create and maintain with Deriv or with a broker that supports cTrader. Chilla connects to that account so your chosen behaviours can run within your authorized boundaries. Beaverly is not the provider, broker, bank, or custodian for the account.

Choose a connection path

PathBest fitSupported asset or behavior categories
Deriv directUse this when you want a direct Deriv connection and Deriv is available for your region and account type.Deriv-native categories such as Multipliers, Options, Accumulators, and similar Deriv-supported products.
cTraderUse this when you already have, or want to create, an account with a broker that provides cTrader access.Categories can include Forex, Metals, Synthetic Indices, Stocks, Crypto, and Stock Indices. Availability depends on the broker and account you choose; not every cTrader broker supports every asset category.

If both paths are available to you, choose based on the provider account you are comfortable opening and maintaining. Chilla does not recommend a broker, provider, account type, asset, or behavior.

Connect an account

  1. Open Pick Connected Account. Use dashboard plus button or Connect Chilla.
  2. Select provider. Current paths are Deriv direct and cTrader.
  3. Authorize provider login. Complete the provider or cTrader authorization flow. Chilla returns to dashboard after callback.
  4. Confirm connection state. Dashboard and account settings should show connected account details.
  5. Set active account. Use Switch Account or account selector controls before creating/updating instructions.

Funding basics

  • Fund your account only through the provider or broker that holds the account.
  • Funding methods, minimums, currencies, timing, and fees are set by the provider or broker, not Beaverly.
  • Chilla may show account readiness, but it does not move money into the account or advise how much to deposit.
  • If an account has no available balance, incomplete verification, or provider restrictions, automation may not be ready even when the connection itself succeeds.

For a focused checklist, see Funding connected accounts.

Withdrawal basics

  • Withdrawals happen inside your provider or broker account, using that provider's own controls.
  • Beaverly cannot approve, speed up, reverse, or guarantee provider withdrawals.
  • Provider verification, payment-method rules, open positions, currency conversion, or regional rules can affect withdrawal availability.
  • If you plan to withdraw, review active instructions first and pause or revoke anything you do not want running while you manage the account.

For a focused checklist, see Withdrawing from connected accounts.

Account FAQ

Can I connect multiple accounts?

Yes, based on your current plan tier. See plans and usage docs for connection allowances.

How do I know which account is active?

Check instruction context and account status cards before activation.

Does Beaverly hold my funds after connection?

No. Funds remain in your own connected account with your provider or broker.

Which assets and behaviors are supported?

Deriv direct supports Deriv-native categories such as Multipliers, Options, Accumulators, and similar products. cTrader availability depends on the broker and account you create, and can include categories such as Forex, Metals, Synthetic Indices, Stocks, Crypto, and Stock Indices.
