Where deposits happen

Deposits happen with the provider or broker that holds your connected account. Beaverly does not receive deposits, hold balances, recommend deposit amounts, or choose payment methods for you.

Before you deposit

  1. Confirm the connection path. Deriv direct and cTrader accounts use different provider experiences for setup and deposits.
  2. Complete provider verification. Identity, region, payment-method, or account-type checks may be required by your provider or broker.
  3. Check the account currency and terms. Minimums, processing times, fees, currencies, and available deposit channels are provider-controlled and often region-dependent.
  4. Compare available channels. Typical paths can include bank transfer, crypto, payment gateways, mobile money, cards, or local payment partners, depending on your broker and region.
  5. Check withdrawal rules first. Before committing to a deposit channel, confirm the withdrawal time and whether withdrawals must return through that same channel.
  6. Choose a practical channel. Use the path that is most convenient, reliable, and fast for your situation after checking the provider's rules.
  7. Return to Chilla after depositing. Confirm the account is connected, ready, and selected before activating styles.

Availability by connection path

PathWhat to expect
Deriv directDeposits, verification, limits, and supported products are handled by Deriv. Deriv-native categories can include Multipliers, Options, Accumulators, and similar Deriv-supported products.
cTraderDeposits and asset availability depend on the cTrader broker and account you create. Categories can include Forex, Metals, Synthetic Indices, Stocks, Crypto, and Stock Indices, but not every broker supports every category.
