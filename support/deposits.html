Deposits

Your provider handles your money.

Beaverly is non-custodial. Chilla may help you navigate a supported provider’s deposit area, but you choose the amount, payment details and final confirmation.

Where deposits happen

Deposits happen with the external provider that holds your account. Beaverly does not receive deposits or custody the balance.

Minimums, supported currencies, payment methods, processing time, conversion and fees are controlled by the provider and can vary by account and region.

What Chilla can help with

On a supported provider journey, Chilla can help you navigate to the relevant deposit area and explain ordinary page context.

Chilla does not choose a deposit amount, select sensitive payment details for you, or make the final money-moving confirmation.

Before you confirm a deposit

  • Make sure you are on the intended provider account.
  • Review the provider’s currency, fees, limits and expected processing time.
  • Check any identity or payment-method requirements.
  • Review withdrawal rules for the payment method if that matters to you.
  • Enter sensitive payment or authentication information yourself.
  • Confirm the transaction yourself.

Credentials and payment information

Do not paste provider passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or sensitive payment credentials into the Chilla conversation. When a sensitive step is required, you take over that step.

Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.

After a deposit

Provider balances and readiness can take time to update. When you return to Chilla, confirm that the correct account is connected and that the account is ready before authorizing live work.
