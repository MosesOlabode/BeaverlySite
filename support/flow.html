Flow and Transparency

The activity trail behind the conversation.

Chilla can give you the answer in plain language. Flow is there when you want to inspect the underlying activity itself.

Flow and Chilla have different jobs

Chilla

Answers your question, summarizes what matters and tells you whether anything needs your attention.

Flow

Keeps the factual activity record available when you want to inspect the work itself.

You do not need to read Flow every day for Chilla to be useful. It exists so transparency is available when you want it.

Common Flow event types

EventPlain-language meaning
EntryChilla started market activity within active authorized work.
ExitPreviously opened activity was closed.
Skip / PassChilla stayed out or did not proceed because the work should not continue in that situation.
WarnSomething about the account, provider, work or environment needs attention or prevented normal progress.
LiveAn ongoing status update without a new entry or exit.

Ask before you dig

If something in Flow is confusing, ask Chilla about it. Chilla can explain the user-relevant reason using the context it is allowed to read.

Flow is not intended to expose Beaverly's proprietary thresholds, security controls, execution internals or private orchestration. It explains enough for the user to understand the outcome and get support when needed.

Quiet Flow can be normal

Automation does not need to manufacture activity. A quiet period can simply mean Chilla has nothing appropriate to do within the active work.

If you expected activity, first ask Chilla what is happening. You can also check the active task, connected account and Work Capacity, then use troubleshooting if something appears wrong.

Inspect, filter and share

Depending on the current Chilla client, Flow can provide filtering, event detail, history and share/export options so you can review a specific period or keep evidence of what happened.

When you contact support, the useful information is normally the approximate time, the user-visible event or warning, the affected account context, and what you expected to happen. Do not send passwords, OTPs or other secrets.

Flow FAQ

Is Flow the same thing as Chilla's explanation?

No. Chilla gives you a conversational explanation. Flow is the underlying user-facing activity record.

Does a skip mean Chilla failed?

No. A skip can be the correct outcome when the active work should not proceed in that situation.

Can Flow expose Beaverly's internal logic?

No. Flow is designed to explain user-relevant outcomes without publishing protected implementation details.
