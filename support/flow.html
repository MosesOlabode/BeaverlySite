The story behind the work.

Chilla reports the work, not a log dump

Report Back turns the important parts of Chilla’s ongoing market work into a concise narrative you can understand without reconstructing the day from raw events.

Repeated observations can be summarized together, while meaningful entries, exits, skips, warnings and changes keep the chain of what happened clear.

What Report Back can cover

What happenedWhat Chilla explains
EntryWhy Chilla started market activity inside the work you approved.
ExitWhy previously opened activity was closed.
Skip / stay outWhat Chilla saw and why it decided not to proceed.
WarningWhat account, provider, market or product condition needs attention.
ChangeWhat changed during the work and how Chilla responded inside the approved boundaries.

Ask Chilla directly

You can ask what happened, why Chilla acted or stayed out, what changed, or whether anything needs your attention. Chilla can answer from the relevant work and context in ordinary language.

Quiet can be part of the work

Ongoing work does not need to manufacture activity. Chilla can watch, decide that conditions do not fit the approved work, stay out, and tell you that plainly.

If you expected something different, ask Chilla what it saw and check the active work, trading account and Work Capacity.

Useful context for support

If something looks wrong, send the approximate time, the affected goal or trading account, what you expected, what Chilla reported, and a screenshot with sensitive information removed when useful.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or other secrets.

Report Back FAQ

Is a skip a failure?

No. Staying out can be the correct decision when the approved work should not proceed in that situation.

Do I need to read every update?

No. Chilla reports the important story in conversation so you can stay informed without monitoring every market event.

Can I ask for more detail?

Yes. Ask Chilla about the period or activity you want explained.
