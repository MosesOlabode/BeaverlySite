Funding

Fund through your provider or broker

Chilla is non-custodial. Add money only through the provider or broker that holds your connected account.

Where funding happens

Funding happens with the provider or broker that holds your connected account. Beaverly does not receive deposits, hold balances, recommend deposit amounts, or choose payment methods for you.

Before you fund

  1. Confirm the connection path. Deriv direct and cTrader accounts are created and funded through different provider experiences.
  2. Complete provider verification. Identity, region, payment-method, or account-type checks may be required by your provider or broker.
  3. Check the account currency and terms. Minimums, processing times, fees, and currencies are provider-controlled.
  4. Return to Chilla after funding. Confirm the account is connected, ready, and selected before activating behaviours.

Availability by connection path

PathWhat to expect
Deriv directFunding, verification, limits, and supported products are handled by Deriv. Deriv-native categories can include Multipliers, Options, Accumulators, and similar Deriv-supported products.
cTraderFunding and asset availability depend on the cTrader broker and account you create. Categories can include Forex, Metals, Synthetic Indices, Stocks, Crypto, and Stock Indices, but not every broker supports every category.
