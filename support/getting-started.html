Getting Started

Start with what you are working towards.

Chilla works towards your goals in the financial markets and reports back. You can begin with a conversation, choose what should run, then use demo or a supported live account when you are ready.

What Chilla is

  • Your financial worker: tell Chilla what you are working towards and it can work towards that goal in supported financial markets.
  • Conversational: ask what happened, change direction, check progress, or understand why Chilla acted or stayed out.
  • Bounded: live work requires your authorization and stays within the boundaries you approve.
  • Transparent: Chilla reports back in conversation, while Flow keeps the user-facing activity trail available to inspect.
  • Non-custodial: Beaverly does not hold your money. Funds remain with the external provider you choose to connect.

What Chilla is not

  • Not a financial advisor.
  • Not a brokerage.
  • Not a custody platform.
  • Not a signal group or copy-trading service.
  • Not unlimited authority over your provider account.

Your first Chilla session

  1. Tell Chilla the goal. Write what you are working towards in your own words.
  2. Answer only the choices that belong to you. Chilla handles compatibility, setup details and explanation. When a material choice such as style, account or market still matters, Chilla brings you a small understandable choice instead of making you learn the whole system.
  3. Choose what you want to run. If you are unsure, Chilla can show one or two straightforward supported options to try and explain how they behave. You still choose.
  4. Review the PLAN. Once the required material choices are settled, Chilla presents the exact work for review.
  5. Approve and activate when ready. Approving the PLAN moves you to the required disclosure. Activation happens only after the disclosure and final agreement for that PLAN.
  6. Use demo or live. You can try the experience in demo or connect a supported live provider account. Chilla does not force demo simply because you are new.
  7. Let Chilla work and report back. Individual market actions can happen inside the authorized work without asking you to approve the same scope repeatedly. Ask Chilla what happened at any time, and open Flow when you want the activity trail.

Provider setup can stay conversational too

When a supported provider journey is needed, Chilla can help you navigate setup and account-management pages. Sensitive steps stay with you.

  • Passwords and OTP/MFA remain yours.
  • Identity/KYC and legal declarations remain yours.
  • OAuth approval remains yours.
  • Transaction details and money-moving confirmations remain yours.
  • Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.

See Connected Accounts for details.

Demo and live work

Demo

Try Chilla without putting a live provider account to work. The same principle still applies: you choose the material work you want to try.

Live

Connect a supported account, review the PLAN and activate the work after the required disclosure. Funds remain with the external provider.

After activation

Chilla can carry ongoing activity inside the work you authorized without turning every individual market action into a new approval prompt.

If the work needs a material change that requires new authority, Chilla brings that change back to you. You can also pause or stop active work.

Chilla, M-II and Flow

  • Chilla: the financial worker you talk to.
  • M-II: Beaverly’s financial execution system underneath authorized live work.
  • Flow: the user-facing activity trail available when you want to inspect what happened.
  • Beaverly: the software company building Chilla and M-II. Beaverly does not hold user funds.
