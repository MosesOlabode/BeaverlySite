Getting Started

Start with what you are working towards

Chilla works towards your goals in the financial markets and reports back. You can begin in demo before connecting anything, then authorize live work through a supported account when you are ready.

What Chilla is

  • Your financial worker: tell Chilla what you are working towards and it can work towards that goal in supported financial markets.
  • Conversational: you can ask what happened, change direction, check progress, or understand why Chilla did or did not act.
  • Bounded: material live work requires your authorization and runs within the boundaries you approve.
  • Transparent: Chilla reports back in conversation, while Flow keeps the underlying activity trail available to inspect.
  • Non-custodial: Beaverly does not hold your money. Funds remain with the external account provider you choose to connect.

What Chilla is not

  • Not a financial advisor.
  • Not a brokerage.
  • Not a custody platform.
  • Not a signal group or copy-trading service.
  • Not a language model with unrestricted broker authority.
Plain-language summary: Chilla can do ongoing work after you authorize the material plan. It cannot silently expand that authority because a chat response said so.

Your first Chilla session

  1. Tell Chilla the goal. Write what you are working towards in your own words. You do not need to build a workflow before Chilla can help.
  2. Review what Chilla prepares. Chilla can turn the goal into a bounded plan using supported styles, markets, comfort and the context available to it.
  3. Change anything that does not fit. Keep the conversation going until the material plan matches what you intend to authorize.
  4. Begin in demo or connect a supported account. Demo lets you understand the experience before connecting an external account. Live work requires a supported connected provider/account.
  5. Authorize live work. Review and approve the material configuration before it becomes active. Chilla can then work inside that scope without asking you to approve each individual market action.
  6. Let Chilla report back. Return to the conversation to ask what happened or what changed. Open Flow when you want the underlying activity record.

Demo and live work

Demo

You can start before connecting an external account. Use demo to see how Chilla interprets the goal, prepares work, behaves around supported market conditions, and reports back.

Live

When you are ready for live work, connect a supported account and authorize the material plan. Funds stay with that provider. Chilla and M-II operate through the authorized connection rather than taking custody.

What happens after authorization

Authorization applies to the material configuration you reviewed. Once that work is active, Chilla can carry out individual market actions inside the approved scope without turning every action into a new approval prompt.

If a material change needs new authority, Chilla should return to you rather than silently changing the active work. You can also pause or stop work through the controls available in Chilla.

How Chilla, M-II and Flow fit together

  • Chilla: the conversational experience where you state the goal, review work, authorize it and ask what happened.
  • M-II: Beaverly’s financial execution system underneath Chilla. It enforces authorization, validates actions, communicates with providers, reconciles results and keeps execution truth outside the language model.
  • Flow: the factual activity trail. Chilla can explain the important part in conversation; Flow remains available when you want to inspect the source activity.
  • Beaverly: the software company building Chilla and M-II. Beaverly does not hold user funds.
