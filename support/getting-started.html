Getting Started

Start with what you are working towards.

Chilla works towards your goals in the financial markets and reports back. You can begin in demo before connecting anything, then authorize live work through a supported account when you are ready.

What Chilla is

  • Your financial worker: tell Chilla what you are working towards and it can work towards that goal in supported financial markets.
  • Conversational: ask what happened, change direction, check progress, or understand why Chilla acted or stayed out.
  • Bounded: material live work requires your authorization and stays within the boundaries you approve.
  • Transparent: Chilla reports back in conversation, while Flow keeps the underlying activity trail available to inspect.
  • Non-custodial: Beaverly does not hold your money. Funds remain with the external provider you choose to connect.

What Chilla is not

  • Not a financial advisor.
  • Not a brokerage.
  • Not a custody platform.
  • Not a signal group or copy-trading service.
  • Not unlimited authority over your provider account.

Your first Chilla session

  1. Tell Chilla the goal. Write what you are working towards in your own words.
  2. Review what Chilla prepares. Chilla can prepare supported style, market, Comfort and other material choices from the goal and available context.
  3. Change anything that does not fit. Keep the conversation going until the material work matches what you intend to authorize.
  4. Start in demo or connect a supported account. Demo lets you understand the experience before live work.
  5. Authorize live work. Approve the material work when you are ready. Chilla can then carry it inside those boundaries without asking you to approve every individual market action.
  6. Let Chilla report back. Return to the conversation to ask what happened or what changed. Open Flow when you want the underlying activity record.

Provider setup can stay conversational too

When a supported provider journey is needed, Chilla can help you navigate setup and account-management pages. Sensitive steps stay with you.

  • Passwords and OTP/MFA remain yours.
  • Identity/KYC and legal declarations remain yours.
  • OAuth approval remains yours.
  • Transaction details and money-moving confirmations remain yours.
  • Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.

See Connected Accounts for details.

Demo and live work

Demo

Start with the goal before connecting an external account. Use demo to understand the Chilla experience first.

Live

Connect a supported account and authorize the material work. Funds remain with the external provider.

After authorization

Authorization applies to the work you reviewed. Chilla can carry individual market actions inside that scope without turning every action into a new approval prompt.

If a material change needs new authority, Chilla returns to you. You can also pause or stop active work.

Chilla, M-II and Flow

  • Chilla: the experience you talk to.
  • M-II: Beaverly’s financial execution system underneath authorized live work.
  • Flow: the user-facing activity trail available when you want to inspect what happened.
  • Beaverly: the software company building Chilla and M-II. Beaverly does not hold user funds.
