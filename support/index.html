Support FAQ

Does Beaverly hold my funds?

No. Chilla does not hold your money. Your funds stay in your connected account.

Does Chilla give financial advice?

No. Chilla works within user-authorized tasks and boundaries. Beaverly provides software technology.

Where can I see account compatibility?

Use the public Compatibility page, then Chilla checks the actual account path when you choose to connect for live work.
