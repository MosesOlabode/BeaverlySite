Support FAQ

Does Beaverly hold my funds?

No. Chilla is non-custodial. Your funds remain with your external account provider.

Does Chilla need my broker password?

Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords. Sensitive credential entry stays with you.

Can Chilla approve deposits or withdrawals for me?

No. Chilla may help you navigate to supported account-management areas, but transaction details and consequential money-moving confirmations remain yours.

Does Chilla give financial advice?

No. Chilla works toward user goals through supported product choices and the boundaries the user authorizes. Beaverly provides software technology.
