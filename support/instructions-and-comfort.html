Tasks and Limits

The work Chilla carries for you.

Start with the goal. Chilla handles the machinery and brings you the material choices that still belong to you. Once those are settled, the PLAN shows exactly what you are considering before anything goes live.

What a task means

A task is a bounded piece of ongoing work Chilla can carry after you activate it. You do not need to build it through a multi-page trading wizard.

Tell Chilla what you are working towards. Chilla can handle retrieval, compatibility and explanation, then ask for the smallest material choice that still belongs to you.

You choose the material work

Behaviour, trading-account and market choices are yours. Chilla can remove options that are not actually available, explain the remaining choices in ordinary language, and keep the conversation small.

If you are new or unsure, Chilla may put one or two straightforward compatible behaviours in front of you to try and explain how each works. That is a simpler choice, not a recommendation or an automatic selection. You decide what to run.

What the PLAN is

Once the required choices are settled, Chilla creates the PLAN: the concise receipt for the material work you are considering.

Depending on the task, it can include the trading account, the way Chilla will work, market scope, how much of the account Chilla may use per activity, and other material limits. Check the Plan before moving forward.

Approving the PLAN is not activation

Approve PLAN means you are satisfied with the work shown and want to continue. Chilla then presents the required disclosure for that exact work.

Activate is the final action that puts the reviewed work live after the required agreement. If a material part of the PLAN changes, review the updated work before activating it.

How much of the account Chilla can use per activity

The Plan shows the amount Chilla may use for an individual activity as a percentage of the selected account, plus the money amount when available.

You can change that amount before activation or later when you want to change the work. It is a limit on an individual activity, not a profitability setting.

Pause, change or stop

Pause

Temporarily stop active work while keeping it available to return to.

Change

Tell Chilla what you want changed. A material change that needs new authority comes back to you for review.

Stop

End active work when you no longer want Chilla carrying that task.

Active does not mean constant activity

Chilla can stay out when conditions do not fit the authorized way of working toward the goal. It does not need to manufacture market activity just because a task is active.

Ask Chilla what happened when you want the short answer. Ask Chilla to report back with more detail when you want it.

Task FAQ

Can I change how much Chilla uses per activity after activation?

Yes. Tell Chilla what you want changed. If the change materially affects active work, review the updated Plan before the new work is activated.

Do I have to know every available way of working and market before I start?

No. Start with the goal. Chilla can search the compatible options from what you said you want and explain a small set in ordinary language.

Can Chilla surface relevant ways of working from my goal?

Yes. Chilla can use the goal and work preferences you explicitly stated to surface compatible options and explain why they are relevant. You can edit the Plan before activation.

Does activating a task mean Chilla can do anything on my trading account?

No. Activation is bounded to the work you reviewed and authorized.

Can Chilla act without asking me every time?

Yes, inside already authorized work. That is what makes the work ongoing rather than manual.
