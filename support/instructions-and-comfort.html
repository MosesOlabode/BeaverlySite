Tasks and Comfort

The work Chilla carries for you.

You start with the goal. Chilla can prepare the supported style, markets, comfort and other material choices for review. You can change them before you authorize live work.

What a task means now

A task is a bounded piece of ongoing work Chilla can carry after the required authorization. You do not have to assemble it from a multi-page wizard before Chilla understands you.

Start by telling Chilla what you are working towards. Chilla can prepare a supported plan for that goal and bring the material choices into the conversation for review.

What you review before live work

The exact details depend on the goal and supported product context, but material work can include:

  • the style Chilla will use;
  • the supported market or markets involved;
  • comfort for the activity;
  • the connected account that live work will use;
  • other assumptions or boundaries that materially change the work.

You can ask Chilla to change the plan before you authorize it.

Authorization is one decision about bounded work

When the work is ready, Chilla asks for the authorization that makes the material configuration live. That authorization does not give Chilla unlimited authority.

Once active, Chilla can carry individual market actions inside that approved scope without asking you to approve every single action. A material change that needs new authority comes back to you.

What Comfort means

Comfort is a user-facing boundary for financial activity. It helps define how much can be put at work for an individual activity inside the task.

Comfort is not a profitability setting and it does not remove market risk. If you change Comfort in a way that materially changes active work, review the updated work before it becomes the new live configuration.

Pause, change or stop

Pause

Temporarily stop active work while keeping it available to return to.

Change

Tell Chilla what you want changed. Material changes that need new authorization return to you for review.

Stop

End active work when you no longer want Chilla carrying that task.

What Chilla may decide while a task is active

Active does not mean constant market activity. Chilla can stay out when conditions do not fit the authorized way of working toward the goal.

You can ask Chilla why it acted or stayed out. Flow remains available when you want the underlying activity trail.

Task FAQ

Can I change Comfort after activation?

Yes. Tell Chilla what you want changed. If the change materially affects active work, review and authorize the updated configuration before it becomes live.

Do I have to choose the style and markets myself?

No. Chilla can prepare supported choices from your goal. You remain free to inspect and change the material plan before authorization.

Does authorizing a task mean Chilla can do anything on my account?

No. Authorization is bounded to the work you approved.

Can Chilla act without asking me every time?

Yes, inside an already authorized task. That is what makes the work ongoing rather than manual.
