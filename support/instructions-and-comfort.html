Tasks and Comfort

How to create, pause, and stop tasks

This page covers the task setup and the comfort controls that define your automation boundaries.

Article type: Task + Reference Last reviewed: March 12, 2026

Where to find this

  • Dashboard card: Tasks -> Tap to create a task opens the setup wizard.
  • Dashboard card settings icon opens Your Tasks (`mandate-settings.html`).
  • Inside Your Tasks, the action bar includes Add, Update, Pause, and Stop.

What you can do here

  • Create a task with selected style and market context.
  • Set Comfort per trade activity and optionally enable Omega Shield Cap.
  • Activate from wizard step 5. Activate.
  • Pause, update, add to, or stop from Your Tasks.
  • Review detailed fields: Styles, Account, Shield Cap (Omega), Comfort per trade activity, Omega enabled, Status, Active since.

Create a task

  1. Step 1 - Styles: choose your desired style.
  2. Step 2 - Markets: choose where the style runs.
  3. Step 3 - Comfort: set Comfort per trade activity; optionally enable Omega Shield Cap and Strict mode.
  4. Step 4 - Confirm: review disclosure text and tick the consent checkbox.
  5. Step 5 - Activate: review summary cards and activate.

Pause or stop a task

Pause

Use when you want to temporarily stop automation while keeping your setup.

  • Current setup remains available for reactivation.
  • History remains available in Flow.

Stop

Use when you want to fully stop the task setup.

  • Stopping requires explicit confirmation in the UI.
  • You can create a new task afterward if needed.

Task FAQ

Can I change comfort after activation?

Yes. Use Update in Your Tasks and review the updated task history in Flow.

Why does my task not move to Active?

Check account readiness, account selection, comfort settings, and consent completion. Then review Flow and activity status for pause reasons.

Is comfort financial advice?

No. Comfort is your own user-defined boundary setting. Chilla enforces what you configure.
