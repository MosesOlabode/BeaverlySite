How usage is shown

Capacity is presented as a percentage. Beaverly keeps the underlying workload counters internal.

  • Capacity available: the percentage of room remaining in the current capacity window.
  • Capacity tier: the amount of automation room included with your current tier.
  • Reset window: weekly for Chill Architect and daily for paid tiers.
  • Connected accounts: the maximum live accounts included with the tier.

Billing basics

  • Paid tiers support monthly and annual billing.
  • Use the billing toggle on Pricing or in the upgrade flow to compare the two cadences.
  • Changing tier does not change the account provider that holds your funds.
  • For billing support, include your account email, tier name, date, and issue summary.

Tiers and billing FAQ

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Chill Architect gives you limited weekly capacity before you choose a paid tier with daily capacity.

Why does Chilla show a percentage instead of a large capacity number?

The percentage is the useful user-facing view. Internal workload units are implementation details and are not needed to manage your tier.

Does a higher tier change the way a style works?

No. Higher tiers provide more capacity room and larger account allowances; they do not turn a style into a different style.
