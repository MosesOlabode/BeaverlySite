How Work Capacity is shown

Chilla presents Work Capacity in a user-facing form such as an available percentage. The internal workload units are not something you need to manage.

When proposed work would meaningfully affect available capacity, Chilla can explain the useful effect before you authorize it.

Billing basics

  • Paid tiers support the billing options shown on the current Pricing page.
  • Changing tier changes product capacity and account allowances, not who holds your market funds.
  • Your provider account and your Beaverly subscription are separate relationships.
  • For billing support, include your Beaverly account email, tier, date and a short description of the issue. Do not send payment secrets or provider credentials.

Tiers and billing FAQ

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. You can begin on Chill Architect and move to a paid tier when you need more room to work.

Does a higher tier make Chilla more profitable?

No. A higher tier changes Work Capacity and account allowances. It is not a promise of better market outcomes.

Why is capacity shown as a percentage instead of internal units?

The percentage is the useful user-facing view. Internal workload units are not needed to understand your tier.
