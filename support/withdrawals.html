Withdrawals

You control money leaving your provider account.

Beaverly is non-custodial. Chilla may help you navigate a supported provider’s withdrawal area, but you choose the amount, destination details and final confirmation.

Where withdrawals happen

Withdrawals are processed by the external provider that holds your account. Beaverly cannot approve, speed up, reverse or guarantee a provider withdrawal.

What Chilla can help with

On a supported provider journey, Chilla can help you navigate to the relevant withdrawal or account-management area and explain ordinary page context.

Chilla does not choose the amount or destination, enter sensitive transaction details for you, or make the consequential confirmation.

Before you confirm a withdrawal

  • Check whether active Chilla work should be paused or stopped while you change the account balance.
  • Make sure you are on the intended provider account.
  • Review provider verification, fees, currency conversion, payment-method and processing rules.
  • Enter sensitive authentication and destination information yourself.
  • Review the amount and destination yourself.
  • Make the final confirmation yourself.

If the provider will not let you withdraw

Provider-side restrictions can involve account verification, payment-method rules, pending reviews, open financial activity, regional rules or other provider requirements.

Contact the provider for account-specific withdrawal issues. Contact Beaverly when the problem is Chilla navigation, connection state or another Chilla-side experience.

Credentials and confirmation codes

Do not paste provider passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or sensitive transaction credentials into Chilla. When a sensitive step is required, you take over that step. Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.
