Account pathways

Use a supported provider account.

Chilla connects only after you authorize the relevant account access. Beaverly does not hold your funds, and the provider relationship remains between you and that provider.

Available

cTrader accounts

Chilla can work through supported cTrader account connections. Availability depends on the provider, region, account type and whether the required account access is available.

If you already use cTrader, Chilla can check compatibility when you choose to connect for live work.

Integration evolving

Deriv

Beaverly also supports Deriv pathways, including work tied to Deriv-native market products as that integration evolves.

Current availability is shown inside Chilla rather than assumed from an older website list.

Provider assistance

Chilla can help you through supported provider pages.

Provider setup and account administration can be confusing. Chilla can help with supported navigation while sensitive and consequential actions stay under your control.

Chilla can help navigate

Supported setup, account-management areas and ordinary provider navigation.

You handle sensitive steps

Passwords, OTP/MFA, identity checks, legal declarations, approvals, transaction details and money-moving confirmations remain yours. Chilla does not store plaintext broker passwords.
