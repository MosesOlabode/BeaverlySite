Account providers

Connect through supported account technology.

Chilla connects to an account only after you authorize it. Beaverly does not hold your funds, and the provider relationship remains between you and the provider.

Active

cTrader account technology

Chilla connects through cTrader Open API. That makes Chilla compatible with supported cTrader accounts from providers and prop firms that expose the required cTrader account access.

Deriv cTrader IC Markets Pepperstone FP Markets FxPro Fusion Markets Tradeview Skilling FTMO

Provider support can differ by region, account type, and whether that provider has enabled cTrader access for your account.

Temporarily paused

Deriv direct integration

Chilla also has a direct Deriv pathway for Deriv-native products. That pathway is separate from cTrader and is currently being upgraded.

Options Multipliers Accumulators

Deriv cTrader accounts are separate from this direct pathway and can still use Chilla through the active cTrader route.

Financial markets

Markets are selected through the account you connect.

Chilla only shows markets that the current style and connected account can actually use. The market family is the useful public view; the exact symbol list stays contextual inside Chilla.

Forex

Currency markets available through compatible connected accounts.

Metals

Supported metal markets such as gold and silver where the connected account exposes them.

Indices

Supported index markets available through compatible account providers.

Synthetics

Supported synthetic markets through compatible Deriv account pathways.
